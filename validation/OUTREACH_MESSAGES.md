# MilestoneX Validation Outreach Messages

## Freelancer message

> Hey! I'm testing MilestoneX, a hackathon prototype for safer milestone payments in remote work. I'm looking for freelancers who completed a paid remote project in the last year. Could I ask you five neutral questions and show you a two-minute prototype? It takes about 5–7 minutes. I won't collect private payment details, and any quote will be anonymous and used only with permission.
>
> Live prototype: https://milestonex-flare.vercel.app/

## Client or agency message

> Hey! I'm testing MilestoneX, a hackathon prototype that lets clients fund project milestones in escrow and release payment after reviewing delivery evidence. I'm looking for people who hired a freelancer or agency in the last year. Could I ask you five neutral questions and show you a two-minute prototype? It takes about 5–7 minutes. I won't collect private payment details, and any quote will be anonymous and used only with permission.
>
> Live prototype: https://milestonex-flare.vercel.app/

## Short WhatsApp version

> I'm testing a hackathon prototype for safer remote-work milestone payments. Have you freelanced or hired a freelancer in the last year? I need 5–7 minutes of honest feedback—criticism is more useful than praise. No private payment details required. Interested?

## Follow-up after agreement

> Thank you. Before we start: may I keep anonymous notes and include a short anonymous quote in the hackathon submission? You can say no and still participate.
