# MilestoneX User Validation Guide

## Goal

Collect honest evidence about whether milestone-based FXRP escrow solves a real cross-border work problem. Do not explain every feature before hearing the participant's current behavior and pain.

## Minimum sample

- 3 freelancers or independent contractors
- 2 clients, project managers, or agency owners

## Eligibility

A participant should have completed or commissioned at least one remote paid project in the last 12 months.

## Consent opener

> I'm testing a hackathon prototype for cross-border project payments. This is research, not a sales pitch. May I record anonymous notes from a five-minute conversation and quote a short sentence without your name?

Record `yes` or `no`. Do not publish names, wallet addresses, employer-confidential information, or payment details.

## Interview flow

### 1. Current behavior

> Tell me about the last remote project where payment timing or trust was difficult.

Follow-ups:

- Who paid whom?
- Was payment upfront, after delivery, or in parts?
- What platform or method was used?
- What went wrong or felt risky?

### 2. Severity

> What was the cost of that problem—time, fees, delayed work, non-payment, or stress?

Avoid suggesting an answer.

### 3. Existing alternatives

> How do you reduce this risk today?

Examples may include platforms, deposits, contracts, invoices, staged payments, or personal trust. Ask what they dislike about the current method.

### 4. Concept test

Show the live site only after the first three questions:

https://milestonex-flare.vercel.app/

Then say:

> MilestoneX lets a client place project value into FXRP escrow, lets the contractor submit a milestone evidence hash, and lets the client release each payment after review. What is your immediate reaction?

Do not defend the product. Ask:

- What is clear?
- What is confusing?
- What feels risky?
- What would you expect to happen during a disagreement?

### 5. Adoption

> In what situation, if any, would you use this instead of your current payment process?

Then:

> What single change would make you more likely to try it?

## Required notes

For every participant record:

- Role: freelancer/client/agency/project manager
- Country or region (optional, broad only)
- Recent remote-project experience
- Main pain in their own words
- Current workaround
- Concept reaction
- Biggest concern
- Most valuable feature
- Likelihood: would use / might use / would not use
- One anonymized quote, only with consent
- Product change resulting from feedback

## Evidence quality rules

- Do not fabricate participants or quotes.
- Do not interview only friends who will praise the idea.
- Preserve criticism.
- Separate what the participant said from your interpretation.
- Do not report five interviews as market validation; describe them as early qualitative feedback.
